# Lesson 08: Introduction to Computer Vision

### Estimated Timing: 60 Minutes

## Lab Overview

In this hands-on lab, you will use Azure AI Custom Vision to build an image classification model that identifies different traffic conditions from highway CCTV images. You will create and configure Custom Vision resources, upload and tag training images, train a multiclass classification model, and evaluate its performance by testing it with new traffic images. This process demonstrates how AI can be used to automatically classify visual data and support traffic monitoring scenarios.

## Lab Objectives

In this lab, you will be able to complete the following tasks:

- Task 1: Create a Custom Vision resource
- Task 2: Creating a New Project in Custom Vision
- Task 3: Upload and Tag Images
- Task 4: Training the Model
- Task 5: Test the Model with New Images

## Task 4: Training the Model

In this task, you will train the Traffic Congestion Classifier using tagged traffic images and evaluate its performance using model metrics, then test the trained model with new images to verify its ability to accurately classify different traffic conditions.

1. Verify that all three image tags (**free_flow**, **congested**, and **heavy_traffic**) **(1)** are available and contain 20 images each. Then, click **Train (2)** to start training the Traffic Congestion Classifier model.

    ![](../images/023.png)

1. In the **Choose Training Type** dialog, select **Quick Training (1)**, and then click **Train (2)**.

    ![](../images/024.png)

1. Wait for the training process to complete. During training, the iteration status displays **Training**.

    ![](../images/025.png)

    > **Note:** Model training can take **5–8 minutes** to complete. Wait until the training finishes before continuing with the lab.

1. After training completes, review the model performance metrics, including **Precision**, **Recall**, and **Average Precision (AP)**, on the **Performance** page.

    ![](../images/026.png)

1. Review the model performance metrics and confirm that the classifier has been successfully trained. Then, click **Quick Test** to test the model with a new traffic image.

    ![](../images/028.png)

1. In the **Quick Test** panel, click **Browse local files** and select a traffic image that was not used during training.

    ![](../images/029.png)

1. In the **Open** window, navigate to the `C:\AllFiles\Unit 3-Lesson 7-Introduction to Computer Vision\traffic_dataset\test` **(1)** folder. Click on one image and press **Ctrl+A** to select all the images **(2)** and then **Open (3)**.   

1. Wait for the model to analyze the image and generate predictions.

1. Review the prediction results displayed under **Predictions**, including the predicted tag and confidence scores for each traffic category.

    ![](../images/030.png)
